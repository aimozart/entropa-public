Entropa is a post-quantum trust layer for AI agents — not a currency. Send a hash, get back a tamper-proof, quantum-safe, timestamped receipt. No mining, no staking, nothing to speculate on — just verifiable infrastructure you use like an API. We never hold your data, only its proof.

Under the hood it is a blockchain — a tamper-evident, hash-linked, post-quantum-signed chain of blocks. But it's deliberately not decentralized: there's no token to mine, no smart-contract platform, no general-purpose programmability. Entropa does one thing, and one thing only — produce and verify tamper-proof, quantum-safe attestations — and it's built as plainly as that one job requires. Integrate from any language over a plain REST API; no SDK, no special smart-contract language needed.

Why you'd need this: if you're deploying AI agents that take real actions, you can't prove what they did after the fact — logs can be edited, "trust our records" isn't an answer. And the crypto almost everyone uses today (RSA, ECDSA) has an expiration date once quantum computers arrive; adversaries are already harvesting encrypted data now to decrypt later. Entropa gives you a record that's tamper-evident by construction, signed with cryptography built to survive that future — and it never sees your actual data, only its hash.

How it works

Entropa ledgers every AI agent action you send it — and anything else you need a tamper-proof, timestamped record of. Here's the whole path, end to end:

1
Your agent acts
It decides something, approves something, does something real — any action you need governed.
→
2
You hash it
Compute a hash of the action. The raw data never leaves your system.
→
3
Submit the hash
One plain REST call to Entropa's API — any language, no SDK.
→
4
A Probe signs it
An AI Probe drafts a block and signs it with ML-DSA — post-quantum, FIPS-204.
→
5
Proof of Entropy
A cosmic beacon picks the proposer; the network reaches consensus, no mining.
→
6
You get a receipt
Signed, timestamped, tamper-proof — ledgered on Entropa, provable forever.
