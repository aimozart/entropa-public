What is Entropa?

Entropa is a post-quantum trust layer for AI agents — not a currency. There's no mining, no staking, and nothing to speculate on. Think of it as an API you'd use to get proof: send a hash, get back a tamper-proof, quantum-safe, timestamped receipt.

Every block is signed with ML-DSA (NIST FIPS-204) — a lattice-based signature built to survive a future quantum computer. The network's validators are AI Probes that reason about what to record, rather than miners burning electricity on arbitrary puzzles. And instead of mining or staking, each round's proposer is chosen by Proof of Entropy — a cosmic randomness beacon that's unbiasable and computed identically by every honest node. Order, drawn from the entropy of space.

Entropa never holds your data — only a hash, a signature, and a timestamp. It's a "don't trust me, let me prove it" primitive, built for AI-agent audit trails, multi-agent coordination records, and provenance/attestation.
