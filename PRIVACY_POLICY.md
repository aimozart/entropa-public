# Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-11-17. The current copy is at https://entropa.space/privacy.

When you run Entropa Gate yourself, **we receive nothing**: it runs on your Linux machine, with your keys and your records, and does not report back to us.

**On our sites:** the contact form (the name, email address and message you choose to send) and standard web-server logs from Google Firebase Hosting and Cloud Run (IP address, time, path) under Google's default retention. No cookies, no tracking pixels, no accounts, no passwords, no payment data. The public explorers show our own records and synthetic data only; their records are append-only, so an individual record cannot be selectively deleted.

We use this only to answer you and keep the sites working and safe, and we do not sell personal data. We share it only with our infrastructure provider (Google Cloud) and with authorities when legally required. Contact messages are kept only as long as needed to answer them. Depending on where you live (for example GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA) you may have rights to access, correct, move or delete your data: use the contact form at https://entropa.space/contact. Custom work is handled under a separate written agreement. These sites are not directed at children. Never send secrets or real personal data through the contact form.
